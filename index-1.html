KOLKI

A dark-red horror world with its own portal, a hostile creature, two large bosses, KORRA and BROMBA, and two special eggs. Built for Minecraft Bedrock on Android and other mobile devices.

  • Pack version 1.0.1
  • Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.50 or newer
  • Script API @minecraft/server 1.16.0
  • KOLKI.mcaddon
DOWNLOAD KOLKI

Open the downloaded file with Minecraft and it imports automatically.

Install

  1. Download KOLKI.mcaddon.
  2. Open it with Minecraft and wait until the import finishes.
  3. Create or edit a world, then activate KOLKI Behavior Pack under Behavior Packs.
  4. Activate KOLKI Resource Pack under Resource Packs.
  5. Enter the world, build an Iron Block portal, light it with Flint & Steel and stand inside.

If an older KOLKI pack was installed, remove it first or make a new world. Otherwise the old cached version may run. Keep cheats on if you want to use commands.

What lives in KOLKI

KOLKI Creature

Hostile mob
  • Hunts the player and attacks in melee
  • Spawns only in KOLKI, never in the Overworld, up to about 9 near each player
  • Guards at boss areas are persistent; wild ones can despawn when you move away
  • Drops 0 to 2 rotten flesh, gives 8 XP

KORRA

Boss · underground cave
  • Lives in a cave with 14 KOLKI Creature guards; never spawns randomly
  • Shows a boss health bar named KORRA
  • Gives 500 XP and always drops a Korra Egg

BROMBA

Boss · dark castle
  • Much stronger than KORRA, in a large castle with 29 guards
  • Shows a boss health bar named BROMBA
  • Gives 1500 XP and always drops a Bromba Egg

Special eggs

Keep an egg anywhere in your inventory: hotbar, bag or offhand. It is never consumed, and its effect starts as soon as it is in there. Keep both and you get both effects.

Korra Egg

kolki:korra_egg
  • Max health goes from 20 HP to 60 HP (10 hearts to 30 hearts)
  • The bonus applies within a second; your base health is not permanently changed

Bromba Egg

kolki:bromba_egg
  • Fire immunity and lava immunity
  • Zero fall damage from any height. Long falls feel like slow falling, which is normal

The KOLKI portal

Build and light

Entering KOLKI
  • Frame made only of Iron Block, shaped like a Nether portal
  • Opening at least 2 wide by 3 tall, at most 21 by 21, and clear inside
  • A small standard portal is 4 by 5 blocks on the outside; corners are not needed
  • Use Flint & Steel inside the frame; the portal opens with a crimson glow
  • Stand inside for about 2 seconds to teleport

Arrive and return

Getting back
  • You land somewhere random and safe, away from water, lava, the boss cave and the castle
  • A new Iron Block portal is built for you at the landing spot
  • Return through the same portal to reach your original Overworld portal
  • Or build and light a new Iron Block portal in KOLKI, which sends you to your last Overworld portal, or near world spawn if it is gone
  • If an iron block breaks, the portal closes

The world and boss sites

KOLKI has Overworld-style terrain with mountains, caves, water and trees, under dark red fog. It is always midnight there, with a red moon in the sky. Your original time returns when you leave. KOLKI sits around X 120000, Z 120000.

Structures are generated when you approach: within about 500 blocks for the cave and 700 for the castle. The spot can shift by a few hundred blocks, so check the chat for exact coordinates or run /scriptevent kolki:locate.

KORRA Cave

Four tall pillars with lights mark the surface. A ladder shaft in the middle leads down to a large domed arena with KORRA on a dais, eight pillars, three tunnels and side chambers. Guards are everywhere.

BROMBA Castle

An outer wall with four corner towers, mid towers and a south gate. Inside: a courtyard and a big keep with an entrance hall, a round boss arena where BROMBA waits, two long corridors, eight barracks rooms and a treasure vault to the north.

Good to know

Limits of a Bedrock addon

  • KOLKI is not a true separate dimension. It is a far-away part of the Overworld made to look different with fog, midnight and a red moon
  • The red moon texture applies everywhere, so Overworld nights also show a red moon
  • The sky and clouds cannot be fully recolored; fog approximates the red sky
  • Creatures spawn through scripts, not spawn rules, so they stay in KOLKI
  • The cave and castle may take a moment to appear the first time you get close

If something goes wrong

  • Structure missing: check the chat coordinates or run the locate command; if still missing, run reset and approach again
  • Eggs or spawn eggs missing: remove the old pack, import the new file and test with /give
  • Portal will not open: Iron Block frame only, opening larger than 2×3 and clear, Flint & Steel used inside the frame
  • Scripts not working: you need Minecraft 1.21.50 or newer with the Behavior Pack active
  • To see errors, turn on Content Log under Settings, then Creator
